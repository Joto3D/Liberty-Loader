Deployment

Ready in four steps

  1. Set up CrossOver

    Install CrossOver 24 or newer, create a Steam bottle and download Helldivers 2 in it.

  2. Download Liberty Loader

    Open the .dmg and drag “Liberty Loader” into your Applications folder.

  3. Open it the first time

    macOS blocks apps from outside the App Store once. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click “Open Anyway”.

    Still blocked?

    Run this once in Terminal:

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Liberty Loader.app"

  4. Dive

    Liberty Loader finds your bottle. Pick a performance preset, drop in some mods and hit Launch.

Field manual

Squeeze more FPS out of your Mac

Liberty Loader recommends a preset for your chip and memory. These tips help on every Mac:

  • Keep High Resolution (Retina) mode off. It renders four times the pixels.
  • Use the D3DMetal backend with MSync on Apple Silicon.
  • Play in fullscreen so macOS Game Mode kicks in.
  • Plug in your MacBook and choose High Power mode where available.
  • Quit browsers and other heavy apps to free unified memory.
  • The first missions after an update stutter while shaders compile. This gets better on its own.

Briefing

Questions

Is Liberty Loader free?

Yes. It is open source on GitHub. CrossOver itself is a paid app from CodeWeavers.

Can I get banned for mods?

Cosmetic mods are generally tolerated, but anything that changes gameplay can get you kicked or banned. Mods are used at your own risk.

Does it work on Intel Macs?

The app runs on Intel and Apple Silicon. Helldivers 2 itself runs far better on Apple Silicon.

What happens when Helldivers 2 updates?

Liberty Loader notices the new game build and offers to disable all mods until their authors catch up. Your mod library is kept.

Can I undo everything?

Yes. “Remove All Mod Files” makes the game folder vanilla again, and “Restore Original Settings” brings back your configs from before Liberty Loader touched them.

Something doesn’t work. Where do I report it?

Open an issue on GitHub and attach the log (Settings → Show Log). Report an issue
